# Art-direction review

Final pass before delivery — review the rendered file, not the timeline:

- **First 5 seconds**: does the hook land without sound?
- **Type**: captions legible on a phone (≥22 px equivalent), safe margins.
- **Pacing**: no shot overstays its narration beat; cuts on motion.
- **Consistency**: one color treatment, one caption style, one music bed
  family throughout.
- **Ending**: payoff/CTA visible ≥2 s; audio fades, doesn't truncate.

Record verdicts as checklist items in the project notes; re-render only what
failed.
